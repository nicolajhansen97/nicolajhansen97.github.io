KICKLINE

Jump. Kick. Stay on the beat.

Kickline is a rhythm runner for iPhone and iPad. Every level is built from its own soundtrack. You jump spikes and kick through crystal walls on the beat, build your combo, and earn a grade for every level.

How to play

Right side of the screen: jump. Hold to keep jumping. In the ship, hold to fly; in the ball, tap to flip gravity.

Left side of the screen: kick. It smashes the green crystal walls that are too tall to jump. When a crystal hangs over a spike, tap both sides together.

On the beat: PERFECT and GREAT hits build your combo, multiply your score up to x4 and bring the melody in.

Questions

How do I remove ads?
Tap No Ads on the home screen. It's a one-time purchase.
I bought Remove Ads, but I still see ads.
Open Settings > Restore purchases in the game while signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.
Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
In the game, open Settings > Privacy settings. On iPhone and iPad, you can also use iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the privacy policy.
A level feels impossible.
Every level is checked to be beatable. Try Practice on the Levels screen: it places checkpoints for you.
